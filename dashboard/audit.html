ReplayLens audit tools

Check recorded AI-agent runs against a policy, or compare two voice-agent recordings. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Back to the report explorer

Phrase filters cannot catch an agent that says a refund was issued without calling the refund tool, or one that refunds $480 with nobody approving it. Paste recorded runs and a policy to see which past runs would have been blocked or sent to a human.

A voice agent can answer correctly and still get worse: slower, mis-heard, or full of text a speech voice cannot read well. Paste two recordings of the same calls (JSONL, one turn per line) to see which stage regressed.

Each line: call_id, turn, user_transcript, agent_transcript, stt_ms, llm_ms, tts_ms, optional first_audio_ms and interrupted.
